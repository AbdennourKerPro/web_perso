2026

Drawing Clouds

Reinforcement-learning sandbox where a policy learns to shape a 2D cloud into a target drawing through local weather actions.

Approach

  • A spatial CNN policy with PPO chooses local condensation or evaporation actions.
  • Curriculum learning and a procedural teacher guide the policy through increasingly variable weather.

Result

Best V3 checkpoint: 0.731 mean score, 0.727 precision, and 0.469 boundary alignment. Filled shapes are strongest; hollow contours remain the main challenge.
